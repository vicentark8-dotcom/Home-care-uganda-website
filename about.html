ABOUT US

An idea held for fifteen years, now a home.

Every part of Home Care Uganda traces back to one memory — and one decision to make sure other children never have to live it.

Home Care Uganda began with a memory, not a business plan. Our founder first imagined it at ten years old, after knowing what it feels like to have nothing to eat and no certainty about tomorrow. That feeling didn't fade with time — it became the reason to act. Fifteen years later, that idea has become a real home and school, built to make sure no child nearby has to carry that same fear alone.

"I know what it's like to sit down at night with nothing to eat and no idea what tomorrow holds. That's the exact reason I started this — so no child near us has to feel that alone."

What began as one person's promise is now growing into an organization: a school for the children in our care, and a wider circle of community outreach, donors, and volunteers who believe every child deserves a home.

Mission

To rescue, shelter, feed, educate, and emotionally support orphaned and vulnerable children in Uganda, while building a community of donors and volunteers who give with love, not obligation.

Vision

A future where every child who passes through Home Care Uganda grows up knowing they were loved, educated, and given a real chance — and where this model expands beyond Uganda to serve children in other countries too.

Approach

Start small, prove it works in Uganda, and grow deliberately — care first, scale second.

Faith

We believe God provides — through people, through community, through patience.

Honesty

Every donation is used for the children. No exceptions, no hidden costs.

Dignity

We care for children in a way that protects their dignity, not just their needs.

Presence

Time and attention matter as much as money — a visit, a conversation, a mentor.

Patience

We are new and growing slowly and honestly, not chasing rapid, unstable growth.

Community

This is not one person's project — it belongs to everyone who chooses to support it.

We know that many people are, understandably, cautious about donating online. Here is what we want every visitor to know:

  • Home Care Uganda is a genuine, newly-formed school and care organization based in Uganda — not a scheme.
  • We are in the process of formal registration as we grow; we'll publish registration details here as soon as they're finalized.
  • Donations go directly toward shelter, food, schooling materials, and children's care.
  • You are always welcome to give time instead of, or alongside, money.
