PROGRAMS

Five ways we care, every single day.

Tap any program below to open it up and see exactly what happens inside.

Program 01

Education — Home Care Academy ⌄

Our own school, built inside the home, so no child misses class for lack of fees.

  • Daily classes from foundational literacy through primary levels
  • School supplies, uniforms, and books funded by donors
  • Teachers who understand each child's background and needs
  • A pathway toward secondary school sponsorship as the program grows

Program 02

Shelter, Food & Daily Care ⌄

A safe roof, warm meals, and basic health care — the foundation everything else stands on.

  • Safe housing and bedding for every child in our care
  • Three meals a day, funded by community and donor support
  • Basic health monitoring and access to clinics when needed
  • Clean water and hygiene supplies

Program 03

Emotional & Spiritual Wellbeing ⌄

Because a full stomach isn't the same as a healed heart.

  • One-on-one time with caregivers for children processing loss or trauma
  • Weekly worship and faith activities open to all children
  • Mentors and volunteers who simply sit and talk with the kids
  • Referral support for children who need professional counseling

Program 04

Community Outreach & Donor Relations ⌄

The outward-facing arm of Home Care Uganda — building the relationships that fund everything else.

  • Engaging local community members, churches, and businesses
  • Keeping donors updated on how their support is used
  • Building partnerships that can help the organization grow responsibly
  • Local fundraising events and awareness drives

Program 05

Volunteer & Time-Giving Program ⌄

For people who want to help but can't give money right now — your time is just as valuable.

  • Scheduled visits to spend time with the children
  • Tutoring, reading, or playing sessions
  • Mentorship for older children navigating emotional challenges
  • Skills-sharing sessions (music, sport, craft, storytelling)
