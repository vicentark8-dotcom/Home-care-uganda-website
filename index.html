WHY WE EXIST

Because hunger tomorrow is a fear no child should carry.

Home Care Uganda began as an idea held quietly for fifteen years — born out of knowing, firsthand, what it feels like to sit down at night without knowing where the next meal will come from. That memory became a promise: no child within our reach should have to wonder the same thing.

Rescue & Shelter

A safe roof, a bed, and daily meals for children who have nowhere else to go.

Education

Our own school inside the home, so learning never stops for lack of school fees.

Emotional & Spiritual Care

Someone to talk to, mentors who visit, and a faith community that reminds every child they are loved.
