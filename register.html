REGISTER A CHILD

Apply for a child to join the Home Care Uganda community.

For parents, guardians, relatives, or community members referring a child who needs care. Every application is reviewed carefully and followed by a personal interview — no child is admitted without one.

1

Submit application

Fill in the form below with honest, basic details about the child's situation.

2

Admin review

Our team reads every application and reaches out within a few days.

3

Interview & home visit

We meet the child and guardian/referrer in person to understand the situation fully.

4

Decision

We confirm whether the child qualifies for a place in our care, based on need and capacity.
